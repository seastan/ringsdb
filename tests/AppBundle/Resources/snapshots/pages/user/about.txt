About
This website is dedicated to providing a fast, powerful and exhaustive deck builder and search engine for cards, along with card descriptions and card images.
This website is optimized for mobile browsing (WebKit).

Search syntax
a search string is a conjunction of one or more
conditions
separated by one or more spaces
a
condition
is either:
a
word
or
expression
or
disjunction
a
criteria
followed by an
operator
followed by a
word
or
expression
or
disjunction
a
word
must match
/[\w\-]/
: letters, digits, dash
an
expression
is any text between
"
a
disjunction
is several
words
or
expressions
separated by
|
a
criteria
is a single letter:
b
– threat
o
– cost
w
– willpower
a
– attack
d
– defense
h
– hit points
c
– cycle
e
– adventure pack
t
– type
s
– sphere
u
– unique
k
– traits
x
– text
f
– flavor text
i
– illustrator
y
– quantity in pack
r
– release date
an
operator
is either:
:
– equals
!
– different from
<
– less than
>
– more than
Special case
r
(release date) uses only the operators
<
(inclusive) and
>
(exclusive) and only understands the arguments
now
or a date YYYY-MM-DD
Search examples
t:ally
searches for all Allies
t:attachment k:trap
searches for every Attachment that has the trait Trap
x:"enemy in play"
searches for all cards with the text "enemy in play"
t:attachment|event s:lore
searches for all Attachments and Events from Lore Sphere
s:lore|spirit w>3
searches for all Lore and Spirit cards with Willpower greater than 3
t:attachment k!item|weapon
searches for all Attachments that are neither item or nor weapon
r<2012-01-01
searches for all cards released up to Jan 1, 2012
